# Sound credits

The game's sound effects are cut, layered and looped from free **CC0 (public domain)** packs on
[OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org). Nothing here needs attribution; they are listed out of courtesy.
`scripts/audio-samples.mjs` downloads them and builds the sounds, `scripts/audio.mjs` normalizes and encodes them.

| [25 CC0 bang / firework SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx) | cannon, explosions, the machine gun |
| [Gunshot Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds) | infantry rifle fire |
| [100 CC0 SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx) | metal and wall impacts, silo doors |

| [Mech Stomp / Step Sound](https://opengameart.org/content/mech-stomp-step-sound) | the mech's footsteps |

The noise under the machine gun is a high-passed slice of [a worn two-stroke chainsaw](https://freesound.org/people/Mihacappy/sounds/807194/) by Mihacappy on Freesound, licensed CC0.

**Needs credit (CC BY 3.0):** the aeroplane's engine is [Airplane Prop Loop](https://opengameart.org/content/airplane-prop-loop) by jakobthiesen, licensed [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The credits screen must name him if the game ships.


The falling-bomb whistle is synthesized. The menu soundtracks (`music_menu.opus` "Steel Sky", `music_menu2.opus` "Be Crushed" and `music_menu3.opus` "Run And Gun") were supplied by the project owner; the main menu has a chooser for them, and starts on the last one picked (or a random one the first time).
