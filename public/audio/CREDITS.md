# Sound credits

The game's sound effects are cut, layered and looped from free **CC0 (public domain)** packs on
[OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org). Nothing here needs attribution; they are listed out of courtesy.
`scripts/audio-samples.mjs` downloads them and builds the sounds, `scripts/audio.mjs` normalizes and encodes them.

| Pack | Used for |
|---|---|
| [25 CC0 bang / firework SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx) | cannon, explosions, the machine gun |
| [Gunshot Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds) | infantry rifle fire |
| [100 CC0 SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx) | metal and wall impacts, silo doors |
| [100 CC0 SFX #2](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2) | impacts, mud, rubble, thunder, engine loops |
| [Sound Effects Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/sound-effects-pack) | flesh impacts, servos, a scream |
| [Male Grunt/Yelling sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds) | soldiers' death screams |
| [Mech Stomp / Step Sound](https://opengameart.org/content/mech-stomp-step-sound) | the mech's footsteps |
| [Mechanical Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-sounds) | steps, servos, doors |
| [Missile sound](https://opengameart.org/content/missile-sound) | rocket launch |
| [Air whoosh](https://opengameart.org/content/air-whoosh) | rocket flight, bomb whistle |
| [rumble fx](https://opengameart.org/content/rumble-fx) | the low end of the big explosions and thunder |
| [Rain (loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable) | rain |
| [Wind](https://opengameart.org/content/wind) | wind |

The falling-bomb whistle is synthesized. The menu soundtrack (`music_menu.opus`) was supplied by the project owner.
